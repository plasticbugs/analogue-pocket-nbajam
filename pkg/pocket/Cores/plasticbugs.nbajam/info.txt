NBA Jam
Midway, 1993 (rev 3.01 4/07/93)

Midway's T-unit board in gateware: the
TMS34010 graphics CPU, the DMA blitter,
two 16-bit frame buffers, and the
Williams ADPCM sound board (6809,
YM2151, OKI6295, DAC).

ROMs are not included. Build the image
from your own MAME nbajam romset with
mra_build.py and nbajam.mra, and copy
it to Assets/nbajam/common/nbajam.rom

* A  shoot / block
* B  pass / steal
* X, Y or R  turbo
* Select coin, Start start

Two credits start a game. A new board
shows CMOS INVALID: press any button.
